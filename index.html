The Three Doors

As you walk home from school, you come across a mysterious house that you've never seen in your neighborhood before. With your curiosity getting the better of you, you decide to investigate. When you get inside, the door locks behind you leaving you trapped. In front of you are three doors, each leading to something unknown.

With no other way out, you must choose carefully. Choose a door and step through.

But be careful... once you choose, there may be no turning back.
